# Mode 1: Live Band Setup

## Route the band and stage monitors

The main mix is the sound the congregation hears. An **auxiliary send**, shortened to **Aux**, is like a second serving tray: you choose how much of each source goes to the musicians independently.

**Confirmed monitor source: Soundcraft Ui16 Aux 1.** The monitor system is one Samson XP800B package, described by the team as an 800W portable PA with a removable 8-channel powered mixer and passive speakers. Confirm the exact rear-panel model label against the [Samson XP800 documentation](https://www.samsontech.com/products/portable-pa/expedition/xp800/) before choosing sockets or settings.

1. Connect band microphones and instruments to their approved Ui16 inputs; see the [mixer patch list](mixer-guide.md). Use a suitable DI for instruments where required: **[TODO: instruments and DI assignments]**.
2. With Samson power off, connect **Ui16 Aux 1 XLR output → suitable line-level cable/adapter → approved Samson line input**. Exact input, cable connectors, and input-level setting: **[TODO — verify before use]**. Do not plug into a Samson output socket.
3. Connect the Samson powered speaker outputs to its matching passive speakers using proper speaker cables. Number deployed and left/right output arrangement: **[TODO]**. Do not split outputs or add speakers without checking compatibility.
4. Start the Samson input and master levels down. Confirm tone controls, effects, speech/music switch, and approved operating levels: **[TODO]**.
5. Follow the [power-on order](quick-start.md). In the Ui16 Aux 1 view, raise only the sources musicians need, then raise the Aux 1 master and Samson levels gradually.
6. Confirm whether Aux 1 is **pre-fader** (independent of main channel level) or **post-fader** (follows it): **[TODO]**. Pre-fader is commonly useful for monitors, but the lead must approve this system's configuration.
7. Verify both monitor speakers receive the intended feed. A single mono input must reach the required outputs; do not assume a stereo input's unused side is automatically copied.

> Do not send a Samson return back into the same Aux 1 path. A loop between mixers can make uncontrolled noise. The normal documented route is one-way from Ui16 to Samson.

## Check the monitor mix

Ask musicians for a brief check one at a time. Start with needed vocals or timing cues, keep stage volume modest, and leave unnecessary sources out. Check for feedback before opening all band microphones.
