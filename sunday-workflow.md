# Sunday Setup & Pack-Down Workflow

## Arrive and prepare the school hall

Left and right mean **your left and right while facing the school stage from the congregation**. We set up in front of the stage; we do not use the school's stage as the performance area.

1. **Turn on the hall lights.** Keep exits clear.
2. At the **school sound desk at the far back-left**, open the cupboard beside it and take out **all three remotes**. Its loose lock can reportedly rotate open: use the normal gentle opening method without forcing it. If it will not open, contact the school/key holder: **[TODO]**. Report the faulty lock for repair.
3. Use the respective remotes to **close the stage curtains** and **lower the projector screen**. Both normally work from the cupboard. Keep people and equipment clear of the moving mechanisms; stop if anything catches.
4. If they fail, check the outlets **on the left of the stage beside the curtains**. These supply the curtain and screen functions. Check the plugs and wall switches; do not touch exposed wiring or force the mechanisms.
5. Return those two remotes to the cupboard and bring **only the Epson projector remote to the front**; it does not reach reliably from the back.
6. Check the projector's **blue power-button light**, used by the team as its power-present check. If dark, check the outlet hidden by the **hanging black curtain at the centre of the rear stage wall**. Ensure it is plugged in and switched on.
7. Turn the projector on from the front. Its remote can be fussy: aim carefully and allow time for a response before retrying. Keep the beam out of your eyes; ask the lead if it still fails rather than climbing to it.

## Set up stage-side power and the Ui16

1. From the floor-level outlet on the **right of the stage**, run the mixer extension over the side wing to the right of the main stage. Avoid sharp edges and protect walking routes. Extension length: **[TODO]**.
2. Connect the **large multiplug**. Turn on its switch and the wall switch; check its indicator light. Use only the approved load/distribution arrangement; a light alone does not prove electrical safety.
3. Put the Ui16 **on the stage-access stairs, about 1-2 metres from the right wing**. If the stairs are absent, use the stage edge in the same area, about waist height from the floor. Keep it stable, dry, ventilated and clear of feet/edges, with no cable strain.
4. This position lets the cables reach the rear media desk and reduces extra speaker/power cable runs. Check reach before changing the location.
5. Take the power supply from the Ui16 box. Seat the connector on the **right side of the unit** and gently secure its screw collar. Connect mains to the large multiplug and power on the mixer. **Speakers and Samson must still be off.**
6. [Connect the tablet](mixer-guide.md). **Mute all inputs and outputs and lower channel, main and Aux master faders fully to minus infinity before connecting audio sources.** Check main and monitor views separately.
7. Insert the labelled [recording USB](sermon-recording.md) and confirm recording availability.

> **Fully down means minus infinity (-infinity), not 0 dB.** On a mixer, 0 dB passes signal at its normal level; it is not silence. Set input gain separately during soundcheck.

## Soundcheck before people arrive

1. Confirm the approved service settings or saved mixer setup with the lead. Do not recall an unknown setup while speakers are live; stored levels can change suddenly.
2. Test each microphone/instrument separately. Ask for the loudest expected speaking or playing level and adjust input gain so it stays clear without red clipping.
3. Bring up the main mix gently. Walk the room to check speech clarity, coverage, and comfortable volume.
4. For a band, build the Aux 1 monitor mix separately at the lowest useful volume. Ask musicians what they need to hear.
5. For videos, test FreeShow and a known recording with different content on left and right. Confirm both sides survive the mono route.
6. Practise muting a microphone, laptop, main output, and Aux 1. Confirm which outputs each mute affects before service.
7. Make a short test recording, stop it and verify playback at a controlled level. Confirm recording readiness again.
8. Confirm batteries/charging, media cues, operator handover, and the lead's approval to begin.

**Ready means:** every required source is clear, no sustained ringing or red clipping appears, all walkways are safe, and the operator can control both mains and monitors.

## Operate during service

- Keep the tablet charged and the mixer page ready. Watch the service and listen, rather than constantly adjusting controls.
- Open microphones only when needed. Bring video audio up smoothly and mute it when playback ends.
- Coordinate cues with the media operator. Disable notification sounds and prevent the laptop sleeping during use.
- If control disconnects, reconnect calmly: audio may keep playing. Do not reboot the mixer during service as a first response.
- Start [sermon recording](sermon-recording.md) just before the preacher comes up; include ministry afterwards, potentially Jenny. Stop after ministry finishes and people begin moving around.
- Record faults and any approved changes for the next team.

## Pack down and return to storage

1. Stop and finalise the recording, then return its USB to the side pocket inside the laptop bag main compartment. Stop playback, lower/mute main and monitor outputs, then switch off main speakers and Samson first.
2. Switch off the Ui16 and remaining sources/network equipment. Disconnect mains safely at the plugs, never by pulling cables.
3. Disconnect signal cables by releasing connector latches. Support plugs; do not yank leads.
4. Coil cables loosely using the team's demonstrated over-under method, which alternates the direction of each loop to avoid twists. Strap and sort by label and cable type.
5. Inspect for damage, dry moisture before storage, and separate faulty items with a clear fault label. Do not put a suspect cable back among working stock.
6. Count gear against the inventory. Pack the Samson mixer and passive speakers according to their locking arrangement; do not force latches.
7. Confirm the school-approved projector shutdown/cooldown, screen/curtain position and remote return procedure: **[TODO]**. Do not abruptly cut projector power.
8. Return items to their assigned places, leaving heavy items low and cables protected. Check the room for adapters, tablets, chargers, and rubbish; lock up through the agreed school contact.
