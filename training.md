# Video Training Library

Start with connection and basic mixing, then learn monitor sends. These are selected manufacturer training resources, checked in November 2026; video access and titles may change. General Ui-series videos can show features absent on the Ui16, so follow the Ui16 manual for model-specific controls.

| Order | Tutorial / resource | What to practise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Official Soundcraft Ui-series getting-started page](https://www.soundcraft.com/en-US/get-started/ui-series.html) | Find the Ui12/Ui16 manual and introductory resources |
| 2 | [HARMAN Ui Video Manual Series](https://help.harmanpro.com/ui-video-manual-series/) — select “Getting Connected to Your Soundcraft Ui Mixer” | Connect a tablet and identify the mixer page |
| 3 | Same [official video series](https://help.harmanpro.com/ui-video-manual-series/) — select “Processing Input Channels With the Ui Mixer GUI” | Understand input gain and channel controls before editing settings |
| 4 | [Auxes & Outputs — Ui12 & Ui16 (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oZXUQi4lxj0), linked by [HARMAN training](https://training.harmanpro.com/enrol/index.php?id=357) | Trace the monitor feed; distinguish channel sends from Aux master |
| 5 | [Configuring FX Sends & Aux Sends With Soundcraft Ui Mixers — official video lesson](https://harman.helpjuice.com/en_US/ui-video-manual-series/configuring-fx-sends-aux-sends-with-soundcraft-ui-mixers) | Build a monitor mix and distinguish effects from monitor sends |
| Optional, experienced operators | [Unlocking Extra Auxes — Ui12/Ui16 (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rgtysKkK3hE), linked by [HARMAN training](https://training.harmanpro.com/course/info.php?id=2273) | Understand optional extra-output routing; no change is needed for this system's Aux 1 setup |

Do not practise resets, firmware updates, network changes, or unknown saved setups during service. A new volunteer is ready to operate with supervision after demonstrating safe power order, basic level adjustment, main/Aux identification, feedback response, laptop mono verification, and pack-down.
