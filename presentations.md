# Presentations, Lyrics & Sermon Slides

## Choose the correct program

| Content | Program | Volunteer task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Presentations and announcements | FreeShow | Prepare and check the day's content before service |
| Live worship lyrics | FreeShow | Follow the worship leader and show the correct words |
| Worship videos when there is no band | FreeShow | Test projection and audio through Ui16 input 11 |
| Sermon slides | PowerPoint | Open the prepared sermon file; sermons are not presented through FreeShow |

## Find and prepare the sermon

1. Open **sermons** on the church laptop desktop. This is a shortcut to the sermons folder in **Documents**, not a separate copy of the files.
2. Find the current sermon PowerPoint. **Sune or Jacques, our pastor, should already have prepared it.** Confirm the correct file with them if several look current; do not guess by filename alone.
3. Old sermons are in the **archive** subfolder inside the same folder. Preserve them; do not delete, move or overwrite files during setup.
4. Open the current file in PowerPoint and preview it. Check that the projector displays the slideshow, rather than private notes or the desktop.
5. Practise switching from FreeShow to PowerPoint before service. Follow the preacher's cues, then return to FreeShow if needed for lyrics or announcements.

## Check sound and recording

Test an actual FreeShow worship video through the [laptop/DI route](worship-videos.md) and Ui16 channel 11. Test PowerPoint audio too if the sermon contains any. Silence notification sounds and prevent sleep during service.

Every sermon is recorded on the Ui16 USB, independently of the program showing the slides. Start just before the preacher comes up and include ministry afterwards. Follow the [sermon recording checklist](sermon-recording.md).
