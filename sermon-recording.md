# Sermon Recording

We record **every sermon**. **Sune edits the recording and uploads it to YouTube each week.** Capture the sermon and following ministry, stop safely, and return the USB to its known location.

## Find and prepare the recording USB

1. Look in the **small side pocket inside the main laptop compartment of the church laptop bag**. Identify the USB by its **ENH Build conference lanyard**.
2. During setup, insert it into the Ui16 **recording USB socket, labelled MEDIA – REC**, not the playback socket.
3. On the tablet, find the recording control **at the top-right above the main speaker sound sliders**. The team reports that it changes from grey/dehighlighted to fully coloured when the drive is recognised.
4. If it remains grey and cannot be pressed, check the socket, then **unplug and reinsert the USB while recording is stopped**. This is a known local fix. Never remove the USB while recording or writing a file.
5. Before service, make a short test recording, stop it and verify playback at a controlled volume. Check space and readiness again afterwards. Do not format the drive or delete recordings to make room without the lead's approval.

## Record the sermon and ministry

1. **Start just before the preacher comes up.** Confirm recording is actively running before the first sentence.
2. Keep the preacher's microphone clear and unclipped. Listen and watch the input/main meters.
3. **Keep recording through ministry after the sermon.** This includes anyone else coming up, potentially **Jenny**. Make sure their microphone is open and included in the main mix.
4. **Stop once ministry has finished and people begin moving around.** Do not stop just because the sermon slides end or the preacher hands over.
5. Wait for recording to stop and file writing to finish. Do not remove the USB or switch the mixer off during this process. **[TODO: confirm completion message or drive activity indication on our unit.]**
6. Remove the USB and return it immediately to the **small side pocket inside the laptop bag's main compartment**, with the lanyard attached. Let Sune know it is ready through the normal team handover. **[TODO: exact handover arrangement.]**

## Understand what is captured

The Ui16 records its **main stereo mix** to USB, according to the [official Ui12/Ui16 manual](https://adn.harmanpro.com/product_documents/documents/4311_1477337027/Ui_UserManual_v3.0_original.pdf). This is audio, not a recording of projected slides or video. Main-mix changes can affect the recording: do not mute or lower the main mix expecting a separate sermon recording to continue unchanged. Confirm recorded levels in the pre-service test.

The tablet controls recording; the file is written to the USB in the mixer. An Aux 1-only source may not be captured, so include the preacher and ministry microphones in the main mix. Ask the lead if the test is poor rather than changing the live mix blindly.

## Fix recording problems

| Symptom | Possible cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Record button grey/cannot be pressed | Wrong socket or USB not recognised | With recording stopped, check MEDIA – REC and reinsert the labelled USB; wait for recognition |
| Available button but no active recording | Recording not started | Press record and check active indication/timer |
| Still unavailable after reinsertion | Full, incompatible or faulty drive | Ask the lead; preserve existing files. Do not format during service. Backup plan: [TODO] |
| Ministry missing | Stopped too soon or microphone absent from main mix | Keep recording through ministry and check microphones. Unrecorded audio cannot be recovered |
